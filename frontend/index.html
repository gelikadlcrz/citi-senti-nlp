Positive

How to read results

  • Results are automatic and can be wrong. The model sometimes lists an aspect the comment doesn’t really discuss.
  • Sentiment is given per aspect, so one comment can be negative about one thing and positive about another.
  • The model labels aspects but doesn’t point to the exact words in your comment.
